Win32/Beastdoor Trojan advice page.

 

    A possibly new strain of the Win32/Beastdoor.202.A  trojan is circulating but unfortunately most virus scanners do not appear to be able to remove it properly.
   

    The files that this strain uses are (Win98):

    svchost.exe  in C:\WINDOWS

    msowvx.com  in C:\WINDOWS\COMMAND

    msuqop.com  in C:\WINDOWS\SYSTEM

 

    If you are having trouble removing this trojan then download TDS-3 from the following website.

    (Download the update as well)

    http://www.diamondcs.com.au/

    TDS-3 has been tested on Win98 and WinXP and was found to remove the trojan thoroughly with a scan of the Windows folder.
