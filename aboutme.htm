MY STORY

 

My name is Andrew Werner. You might say I have a grudge against religion.

You might be thinking that I've been hard done

by religion or had something terrible happen to me.

Not so.

I'm just one who started asking questions and searching for the truth.

Believe it or not I was a practicing Catholic not too long ago. But I

started hearing bad things about the Catholic church, and

being an open minded individual I decided to make my own investigations.

I started reading and searching, gathering info. Asking others about their experiences

and knowledge. But most importantly I started studying spirituality and God.

Also I looked at other religions and their beliefs.

This website is part of the result of that personal investigation.

I no longer consider myself a Catholic or even a Christian.

If I'm no longer a Christian then you might ask what am I now?

The answer is I'm now a spiritual truth seeker and metaphysics researcher.

I have no intention of belonging to any religion but I have taken on a keen interest

in virtually all of the spiritual related philosophies, general philosophies and wisdoms, as

well as science. Because I believe that they all have better wisdoms and truths to offer.

If you are a person of faith then I hope you are not offended to any great degree by

 the contents of this website, however sometimes some of us need to be

offended a bit to shake us out of our comfortable delusions.
